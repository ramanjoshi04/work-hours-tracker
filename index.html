How this app works

Log each shift with day, start and end time - hours calculate automatically against the 24-hour/week off-campus cap. Toggle "Scheduled break week" during winter/summer break when the cap doesn't apply. Co-op hours are authorized separately and don't count toward this limit.

Editing shifts

Tap Edit on any shift to load it into the form, change it, and tap Save Changes. Tap X to delete.

Your data
